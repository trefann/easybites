The EasyBites code

What EasyBites believes

Four rules we never break.

• The crunch rule •

No quiet bites

Crunch should interrupt the conversation.

Crisp outside, full-volume inside. If the next table cannot hear it, we are not done yet.
• The sauce rule •

Leave evidence

Sauce belongs on the bite. And probably your sleeve.

Sweet, smoky, fiery or glossy—every sauce earns its place by making the last bite as loud as the first.
• The comfort rule •

Bad-day insurance

Comfort food with main-character energy.

Burgers, fries and chicken packed into a box that understands the assignment before you even open it.
• The home rule •

Made here. Craved everywhere.

Bengaluru born. Craving raised.

Global flavour, local appetite and the unmistakable EasyBites habit of adding a little extra.
